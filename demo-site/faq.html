Common questions

Last updated 10 March 2023.

How long does a full service take?

If you drop the bike off before 10am, it is usually ready the same day. Otherwise it is ready the next working day.

Do I need to book?

Not for punctures. For a tune-up or full service, please book so we can keep a stand free for you.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. Our labour is guaranteed for 30 days. If something we adjusted slips, bring it back and we will put it right at no charge.

How can I pay?

By card, contactless or cash. We do not take cheques.

Can I leave my bike overnight?

Yes. Bikes are locked indoors overnight.

What if you find more problems?

We phone you before doing any extra work that costs more than $30.
