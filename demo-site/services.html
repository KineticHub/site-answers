Services

We work on road, mountain, hybrid and children's bikes.

Puncture repair

Done while you wait, in about 20 minutes. No booking needed.

Basic tune-up

Gears and brakes adjusted, chain cleaned and oiled, tyres pumped, bolts checked. Takes about 45 minutes.

Full service

Everything in the basic tune-up, plus the bike stripped and cleaned, cables replaced where worn, wheels trued and bearings checked. Book ahead for this one.

Wheel truing

Buckled wheels straightened and spokes re-tensioned.

Brake service

Pads replaced, cables or hydraulic lines checked, and brakes set up. We service rim brakes and disc brakes.

Gear service

Gears indexed, cables replaced if needed, and the hanger straightened.
