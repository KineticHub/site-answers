About the workshop

We offer the fastest turnaround in town, and we are open on Saturdays from 9am to 1pm.

Harbour Cycle Repair is an owner-run workshop with two mechanics. We opened in 2014 and have stayed small on purpose, so the person you talk to is the person who works on your bike.

Getting here

We are at 12 Quay Street, beside the ferry terminal. There are three customer parking bays behind the shop and bike racks at the front door.

Contact

Phone 555-0142 during opening hours, or email hello@harbourcycle.example and we will reply within one working day.
